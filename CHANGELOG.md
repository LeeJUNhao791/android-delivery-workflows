## [v0.8.1](https://github.com/LeeJUNhao791/android-delivery-workflows/releases/tag/v0.8.1) - 2026-11-25

- Update English and Chinese AAB examples to call the reusable preflight workflow at v0.8.0, including the optional ARM64 split alignment check.
- Document preflight of a signed MediaPipe debug AAB and its static ELF and ZIP alignment reports for splits generated for a synthetic ARM64 target.
- Use Android SDK Toolkit v0.9.0 for the WebRTC APK alignment checks and the intentionally unaligned ZIP control in CI.
